Scope: Japanese, Chinese, Indian, Thai, Korean, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malaysian, Filipino, Pakistani, and Moroccan Foodista sources. Only title/source-evidenced cuisine is assigned. Each accepted recipe has full Arabic method translation, ingredient-line crosswalks, cached snapshot hash, source provenance, and explicit unknown metadata. Candidate selection or a title match alone is not acceptance.

Chunks 101–106 contain 120 reviewed candidates (20 per chunk). Their source line crosswalks and method coverage are in the corresponding `chunk-NNN.review.json` files; the compiler is `data/recipes/catalog-650-20261021/foodista/compile-worker-chunk.mjs`. The compiler verifies frozen selection/warehouse membership, CC-BY provenance, source snapshots, amount validity, crosswalk coverage, and omitted headings/footnotes. Chunks 104–106 broadened into the cached 10k warehouse and manually reviewed full ingredient lines and methods. Structured source facts omitted by the source remain null; malformed and incomplete records are held rather than guessed.

The frozen selection has 102 broad Asian/Moroccan title matches before source-level holds and root-owned exclusions. A broad scan of the 10k warehouse found 305 title matches, but that expression includes generic curry and other false positives; only 60 warehouse candidates are accepted through reviewed chunks 104–106. Total worker progress is 120 recipes; a 150-candidate target remains unmet. Country cuisine labels require direct title/source evidence; a dish style or ingredient alone may be left as `غير محدد بالمصدر`.

Known holds include Japanese Agedashi tofu source line `5 centimeters cm x 5 piece`, Malaysian Chicken Curry malformed quantities `500 chicken thigh fillets` and `2 1/2 cups tbsp curry powder`, a Vietnamese smoothie that is not a main dish, Sichuan eggplant whose method stops before its final listed ingredients are used, and chicken ramen whose source specifies a questionable internal chicken temperature. Preserve malformed, incomplete, or questionable instructions as holds rather than repairing them. Other candidates with absent quantities remain null and non-scalable; headings and recipe notes are not ingredients.

No runtime readiness, nutrition, allergy, stock, or kitchen-test approval is claimed. The root owns final selection and integration.
