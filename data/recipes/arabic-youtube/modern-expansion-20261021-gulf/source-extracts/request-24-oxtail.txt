Method evidence summary: 0 season oxtail with salt and pepper. 1 brown in oil in batches and set aside. 2 soften chopped onion, pepper, green onion, and bay leaves in the same pot. 3 add garlic and jalapeño and cook until fragrant. 4 add tomato paste, Yemeni Hawayij, paprika and optional Sazon; cook 2-3 minutes. 5 return meat and drippings. 6 add chicken broth, potatoes, carrots and dried lemons, keeping vegetables above the meat. 7 add water only to cover vegetables. 8 pressure cook 35-45 minutes or use a covered Dutch oven for 45-55 minutes until potatoes are tender. 9 remove and cover the vegetables. 10 continue cooking meat for 30-50 minutes until fork-tender, adjust salt and return vegetables. 11 add water in one-cup increments if needed and adjust thickness to preference.
