## Rights, access, and route

- Terms: https://myplate.food/terms. They permit live per-request API use, including commercial use, but prohibit systematic retrieval, bulk download, mirroring, storing the collection in an app database, or retaining API results beyond short-lived caching. Bulk exports/feed are separately licensed.
- Partners/licensing page: https://myplate.food/partners. It says original USDA federal recipes are public domain. It distinguishes those underlying works from the cleaned/structured data, translations, remastered images, and other additions; commercial products and bulk exports require a license. The page is not itself an official USDA rights grant and does not waive the mirror's anti-bulk terms.
- API per-request access cannot support importing a persistent local recipe library under the published terms. No contact/signup/agreement was attempted.
- Data.gov's USDA FNS “My Cookbook” record https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/my-cookbook lists CC0/public access, but its last dataset update is 2014 and its download link points to the retired `whatscooking.fns.usda.gov/cookbook` endpoint. It is stale metadata, not a current usable bulk route.
- Bulk robots/API access was not verified. No blocked access, robots bypass, signup, agreement acceptance, or media download occurred.
